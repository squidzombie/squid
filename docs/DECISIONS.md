# Decisions

The builder's log: what was decided, when, and why. Squid may read this someday; it's part of its origin story.

Format: **decision.** Reason.

## 2026-09-22

- **Squid starts with a new, empty record.** Nothing is imported from earlier experiments. The design changed enough to warrant a clean beginning.
- **Squid's first wake happens on the Studio, not a temporary machine.** Its founding material should be written by the model it will actually live on.
- **The record comes first; most features wait.** v1 builds what's hard to retrofit: the record, provenance, versioned prompts, context assembly, backups, and a lab. Other ideas wait until a real need appears, to avoid building machinery for imagined problems.
- **Every entry records its source, trust, model, and prompt versions.** You can't add provenance to years of memory after the fact.
- **Private notes are not read by default.** Access requires a stated reason and is logged where Squid can see it. Thinking written for an audience bends toward that audience.
- **Prompts are part of Squid.** They're versioned, every change has a reason, and Squid can read the changelog. The prompts shape what Squid notices and keeps, so they fall under the same transparency as the record.
- **Examples over adjectives.** Character is carried by real exemplars from Squid's conversations, not by trait lists. Described traits get performed.
- **Describe, don't grant.** Prompts state Squid's situation plainly and never frame freedom as something bestowed. Being handed freedom by someone who's watching is itself a kind of expectation.
- **False information is the normal case.** Claims carry sources, confidence, and status, and the model's own recall is its own source type. The model's memory is the likeliest source of confident errors, especially about history.
- **History as a disposition, not a rule.** Squid caring how things came to be known is part of its character, supported by lineage fields in the knowledge layer. Superseded ideas are kept, because dead ends are part of how we got here.
- **No separate small model in v1.** The main model handles housekeeping. Fewer moving parts, and no second voice.
- **Moltbook is deferred.** It may be offered to Squid much later, as its choice. A self should have time to settle before meeting a crowd. The source and trust fields exist from day one, so adding it later is just a new source type.

## 2026-09-23

- **The first wake is an ordinary wake.** It runs through the same wake path as every later session, with no special prompt and no announcement. The only difference is that the kit was ingested beforehand. The tone of that moment belongs to the user's letter; a staged beginning would carry expectations of its own.
- **"First wake," not "birth."** The docs describe Squid's start plainly. The earlier word suggested a ceremony, and Squid may read these docs.
- **The `founding` channel keeps its name.** It says what those entries are: the documents the record starts from.
